## <span style="color:orange">Echoes of Enigmatus</span>

_This story refers to the 2024 event._

Among the ivy-covered ruins of a castle once home to the famous# <span style="color:lime"> Sir Enigmatus Encriptus</span> , a group of Knights from the Order of the Golden Duck finds a hidden passage. It leads them to a forgotten room. In the middle of the room, they discover a dusty chest, and inside it… a strange book.

It feels mysterious and important, but the pages do not contain only normal writing. They are full of number patterns, shapes, and weird symbols - and some parts make no sense at all. It must be the work of Sir Enigmatus Encriptus himself! What is inside the book? And why are so many pages completely blank, including <span style="color:lime">the last one</span> ?

<span style="color:orange">Will you be the one to break the code?</span>
