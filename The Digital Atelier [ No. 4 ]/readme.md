## The Digital Atelier

This story refers to the 2024 event.
The Princess of Algorithmia is getting married! The grandest figures from every kingdom will arrive for the celebration, and the King wants this day to be remembered for many years to come.
The right artistic setting is essential if the occasion is to feel truly special. Even the smallest details must be designed with originality, yet remain faithful to the art of algorithms. For this reason, numerous competitions have been announced to create new decorations for the castle and attractions connected with the celebration.
You decide to present your ideas . Who knows? Fortune may smile upon you, and a pouch of gold coins may soon be weighing down your pocket.
Get creating!
